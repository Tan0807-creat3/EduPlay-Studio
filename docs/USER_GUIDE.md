## 1. Introduction
EduPlay Studio is a powerful desktop application designed for teachers and educators to create engaging learning experiences without needing to code.

## 2. Getting Started
Launch the application by running `python eduplay_studio/app.py` or using the provided executable. On the home screen, you can see your recent projects or create a new one.

## 3. Creating Your First Project
Click on **"New Project"** to start. You will need to:
- Give your project a **Name**.
- Choose a **Game Template** (Quiz Classic, Quiz Millionaire, or Fishing Game).
- Add a brief **Description**.

## 4. Using Edubot (AI Assistant)
Edubot is your AI companion that helps you generate questions quickly.
- Open the **Chat** panel.
- Ask Edubot to "Create 5 multiple choice questions about photosynthesis".
- Review the generated questions and click "Apply" to add them to your project.

## 5. Managing Questions
In the **Editor** screen, you can:
- Add, Edit, or Delete questions.
- Change question types (Multiple Choice, True/False, Fill in the Blank, etc.).
- Add images or sounds to specific questions.
- Preview how a single question looks in the game.

## 6. Game Templates
- **Quiz Classic**: A standard quiz format with various question types.
- **Quiz Millionaire**: A high-stakes quiz inspired by the famous TV show.
- **Fishing Game**: A fun, interactive game where players catch fish by answering correctly.

## 7. Preview and Export
- **Preview**: Click the **"Preview"** button at any time to play your game in a built-in browser.
- **Export to HTML5**: Generates a folder with all assets, ready to be uploaded to a website or used offline.
- **Export to Native**: Creates a standalone version that runs on your computer.

## 8. PowerPoint Integration
You can use your EduPlay games directly inside PowerPoint:
- Go to **Settings** -> **Add-in**.
- Click **"Install / Repair Add-in"**.
- Restart PowerPoint and find the **EduPlay** tab to insert your games into slides.
